Devlog

Development notes, features and progress reports from Bullzar Games.

  • 100 generated city levels, a dragon with no skeleton, and music synthesized from numpy (Godot 4 devlog)

    Bao Boom is a 3D arcade game in Godot 4.4: you are a small dragon in a lantern-lit Chinese town the night before a festival. The guards stole the celebration, and you light every firework on the map before they catch you. 100 levels, 10 districts, one-touch controls, a tail that keeps growing. These are the technical problems that were not simple.
